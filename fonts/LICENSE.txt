P052-Roman.woff, P052-Italic.woff, P052-Bold.woff

P052 is URW's metric-compatible Palatino clone from the URW++ base35 fonts
(https://github.com/ArtifexSoftware/urw-base35-fonts), licensed under the
GNU AGPL v3 with the font exception. Re-saved as WOFF, unmodified otherwise,
from the copies bundled with the talk (talks/v2-43/shared/fonts). The page
uses them only on machines without Palatino (css/site.css).
